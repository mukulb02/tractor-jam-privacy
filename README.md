# Privacy Policy for Tractor Jam

Last updated: 2026-09-21

Tractor Jam does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information.

- The game works entirely on your device and does not need an internet connection.
- The only data saved is your current level number, stored locally on your device. It is not sent anywhere.
- The game contains no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking.
- The game requests no special permissions.

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the change is released.
